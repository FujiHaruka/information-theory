      Mathlib-gap: condExp commutes with measure-preserving transforms #

      For a measure-preserving T : Ω₁ → Ω₂ and n a sub-σ-algebra of m₂, (μ₂[f | n]) ∘ T =ᵐ μ₁[f ∘ T | n.comap T].
