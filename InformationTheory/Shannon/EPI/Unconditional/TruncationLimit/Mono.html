      source
      {Ω : Type u_1} [MeasurableSpace Ω] (W V : Ω → ℝ) (Q : MeasureTheory.Measure Ω) [MeasureTheory.IsProbabilityMeasure Q] (hW : Measurable W) (hV : Measurable V) (hWV : ProbabilityTheory.IndepFun W V Q) (hW_ac : (MeasureTheory.Measure.map W Q).AbsolutelyContinuous MeasureTheory.volume) (hW_ent : MeasureTheory.Integrable (fun (x : ℝ) => ((MeasureTheory.Measure.map W Q).rnDeriv MeasureTheory.volume x).toReal.negMulLog) MeasureTheory.volume) :
      differentialEntropyExt (MeasureTheory.Measure.map W Q) ≤ differentialEntropyExt (MeasureTheory.Measure.map (fun (ω : Ω) => W ω + V ω) Q)

      Finite-entropy monotonicity of differential entropy under independent addition: for W a.c., W ⊥ V, and Q.map W of finite differential entropy (hW_ent), h(W) ≤ h(W+V). The proof uses per-fibre translate Gibbs and splits on finiteness of ν = W+V (finite branch: real-valued Gibbs; ⊤ branch: le_top). It requires no truncation, so it applies directly to an un-truncated W.
