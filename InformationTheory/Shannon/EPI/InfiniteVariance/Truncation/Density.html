    Single-component dominating bound for the conditioned marginal density: for a fixed positive-mass n₀ and any n ≥ n₀, the conditioned density p_n := (condTrunc.map Z).rnDeriv volume |>.toReal is bounded by C_Z · pZ, where pZ := (P.map Z).rnDeriv volume |>.toReal and C_Z := ((P.map Z) {|r| ≤ n₀})⁻¹.toReal.
