    Han's inequality — Shearer's inequality (integer covering form) #

    If S : ι → Finset (Fin n) covers each i : Fin n at least k times, then k · H(X_{[n]}) ≤ ∑_j H(X_{S_j}).

    Main statements #

    • shearer_inequality — the integer-covering Shearer inequality. The proof combines the subset chain rule jointEntropySubset_chain_rule, conditional-entropy monotonicity condEntropy_subset_anti, and jointEntropySubset_univ, then swaps the double sum to expose the covering multiplicity cover i := #{j | i ∈ S j} ≥ k.
