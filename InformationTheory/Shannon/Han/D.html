    Han's inequality — subset joint entropy #

    Joint entropy H(X_S) over an arbitrary subset S : Finset (Fin n) of coordinates, with the subset chain rule, conditioning monotonicity, and the subset form of Han's inequality.

    Main definitions #

    • jointEntropySubset μ Xs S — the joint entropy of the (i : ↑S) → α-valued family.

    Main statements #

    • jointEntropySubset_univ — agrees with jointEntropy μ Xs when S = univ.
    • jointEntropySubset_chain_rule — the subset chain rule H(X_S) = ∑ i ∈ S, H(Xᵢ | X_{S ∩ {j < i}}).
    • condEntropy_subset_anti — conditioning monotonicity: T₁ ⊆ T₂ makes the conditional entropy smaller.
    • han_inequality_subset — the subset form (|S| − 1) · H(X_S) ≤ ∑ i ∈ S, H(X_{S \ {i}}).
