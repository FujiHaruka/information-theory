      AWGN Gaussian-input MI closed form #

      The hypothesis-free Gaussian-input mutual-information closed form I = (1/2)·log(1 + P/N) (no opaque h_bridge hypothesis for the textbook identity I = h(P+N) − h(N)), assembled from the MI decomposition (mutualInfoOfChannel_toReal_eq_diffEntropy_sub, ContChannelMIDecomp.lean) and the output-Gaussian bind/conv bridge (AWGN/BindConvolution.lean).

      ContChannelMIDecomp.lean's own closed-form producer awgn_mi_gaussian_closed_form_of_out still leaves IsAwgnOutputGaussian standing as a hypothesis; this file discharges it inline from the AWGN-specialized, hypothesis-free bind/conv fact isAwgnBindEqConv (BindConvolution.lean), so it is the join point where the fully hypothesis-free wrapper is assembled.
