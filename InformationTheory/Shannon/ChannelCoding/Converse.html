      Shannon's noisy channel coding theorem (converse, n-letter i.i.d. form): under a Markov chain Msg → encoder ∘ Msg → Y^n and an i.i.d. joint distribution assumption,

      log |M| ≤ n · I(X_0; Y_0).toReal + h(Pe) + Pe · log(|M| - 1)

      where X_0 ω := encoder (Msg ω) 0, Y_0 := Ys 0, and Pe := errorProb μ Msg Y^n decoder.
