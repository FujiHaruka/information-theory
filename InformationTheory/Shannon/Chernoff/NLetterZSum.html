    n-letter Chernoff Z-sum factorization #

    The n-th power of the single-letter Chernoff partition function chernoffZSum P₁ P₂ lam factorizes as a sum over Fin n → α of per-coordinate tilt factors:

    (chernoffZSum P₁ P₂ lam) ^ n = ∑ x : Fin n → α, ∏ i, (P₁ (x i))^(1-lam) · (P₂ (x i))^lam.

    This is the pmf-level (Fintype) analogue of the finite Measure.pi tilt factorization MeasurePiTiltedFactorization.pi_tilted_sum_eq_pi_tilted, and serves as the normalization for the n-letter change of measure.
