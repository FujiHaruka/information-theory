    Marton's inner bound — exchanging the two receivers #

    The Marton data (pV, K, W) is symmetric in the two receivers: exchanging the two auxiliary letters of pV and K and the two outputs of W produces Marton data for the same channel read the other way around. This file records that symmetry at the level of the five-variable law and of the three informations of the region inequalities, so that a statement proved for the first auxiliary alphabet transfers to the second one by reindexing rather than by a mirrored proof.

    Main statements #

    • martonJointDistribution_swap — the five-variable law of the exchanged data is the pushforward of the original law along the map exchanging both auxiliary letters and both outputs.
    • martonInfo₁_swap / martonInfo₂_swap — the two receiver informations trade places under the exchange.
    • martonInfoV₁V₂_swap — the auxiliary dependence is unchanged by the exchange.

    theorem InformationTheory.Shannon.BroadcastChannel.Marton.martonJointDistribution_swap {α : Type u_1} {β₁ : Type u_2} {β₂ : Type u_3} {V₁ : Type u_4} {V₂ : Type u_5} [MeasurableSpace α] [MeasurableSpace β₁] [MeasurableSpace β₂] [MeasurableSpace V₁] [MeasurableSpace V₂] (pV : MeasureTheory.Measure (V₁ × V₂)) [MeasureTheory.IsProbabilityMeasure pV] (K : ProbabilityTheory.Kernel (V₁ × V₂) α) [ProbabilityTheory.IsMarkovKernel K] (W : BCChannel α β₁ β₂) [ProbabilityTheory.IsMarkovKernel W] :
    martonJointDistribution (MeasureTheory.Measure.map Prod.swap pV) (K.comap Prod.swap ⋯) (ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.map W Prod.swap) = MeasureTheory.Measure.map (fun (q : V₁ × V₂ × α × β₁ × β₂) => (q.2.1, q.1, q.2.2.1, q.2.2.2.2, q.2.2.2.1)) (martonJointDistribution pV K W)

    The five-variable law of the Marton data with the two receivers exchanged — the two auxiliary letters of pV and K and the two outputs of W traded — is the pushforward of the original law along the map that trades the two auxiliary letters and the two outputs.
