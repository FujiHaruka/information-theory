    Broadcast channel — superposition achievability setup and infrastructure #

    Cover–Thomas §15.6.2 superposition coding. The structural setup: the per-coordinate joint distribution and its i.i.d. ambient measure, the auxiliary-variable informations, the two-tier (cloud / satellite) random codebook, the i.i.d. coordinate facts and positivity of the BC ambient law, the (U, X) marginal factorization, typical-set relabeling invariance, the two exponential ingredients of the covering bound, the conditional-slice satellite typicality bound, and the two-tier decoders assembling the broadcast code.
